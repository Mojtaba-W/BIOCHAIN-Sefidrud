🐜 ACO — pathway search

Artificial ants explore a graph. Good candidate routes receive more pheromone; evaporation prevents every early route from dominating. The scientific translation is distributed path search, not literal ant behavior in a river.

LIVE PRINCIPLE · explore → reinforce → evaporate → converge

🐟 PSO — hotspot search

Particles move through an objective landscape using inertia, personal best and global best. Here the landscape represents a conceptual sediment-risk objective built from mapped inputs.

LIVE PRINCIPLE · move → evaluate → remember → converge
Why two algorithms? ACO is naturally visualized as network/path reinforcement; PSO as continuous spatial search. In a real study, both would require a defined objective function, spatial data, constraints, calibration/validation and sensitivity analysis before any management recommendation.

From Nature's Principles to a Testable Framework

The final synthesis connects four evidence layers: Earth observation detects spatial change; hydrology & sediment data describe process; ACO/PSO search candidate pathways or priority zones; and Iranian watershed knowledge supplies local constraints and practices. The output is a decision-support hypothesis that must be tested against observations.
